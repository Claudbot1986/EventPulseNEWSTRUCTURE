Data protection / data residency

All user data is stored in the EU (Stockholm, AWS eu-north-1) via Supabase. We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure provider. For the full privacy policy, see /privacy.

Data protection officer (GDPR)

EventPulse is operated as a small one-person project and is not required to formally designate a Data Protection Officer (DPO) under GDPR Art. 37. For questions about your rights (access, rectification, erasure, data portability), contact hej@eventpulse.nu — we typically respond within 7 days.
