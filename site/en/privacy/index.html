Privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. EventPulse is built on one principle: collect as little as possible, sell nothing and share nothing with advertisers. This policy describes what actually happens to your data — in the app and on this website.

1. Who we are

EventPulse is a personal event agent delivered as an iOS and Android app, operated by Tomor Gashi in Stockholm, Sweden. The app helps users in the Stockholm region find concerts, exhibitions and other events based on their own taste. Privacy contact: hej@eventpulse.nu.

2. What we collect

The website (eventpulse.nu) sets no cookies, no sessions and no tracking pixels. It reads events directly from our database via public REST and renders them in your browser. No statistics are collected on the website.

The app stores an anonymous user profile. You can use the app fully without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple ID or e-mail (magic link) we store:

  • Your e-mail address (if you sign in via magic link).
  • Your Apple user id (if you sign in with Apple — we receive a unique id only, not your e-mail, if you choose to hide it).
  • An automatically generated anonymous user id (if you use the app without signing in).

Your user-generated data includes:

  • Events you save, tap on or mark as uninteresting.
  • Venues and artists you follow.
  • Push subscriptions (if you want notifications for specific venues).
  • Conversations you have with the app's built-in event agent.

The app also collects a hashed device id (djb2, 64 characters) derived from your user id or your anonymous id. It is used only to count users and sessions and cannot be used to contact you. We do not collect any location data: the app never requests location access. Separate, anonymous statistics (for example which events are shown) only happen if you opt in under Profile → Privacy in the app, and are retained for up to 30 days.

3. Why we process the data

Account data and user-generated data are processed to deliver the service you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b), contract). Push notifications and anonymous statistics rely on your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in the app. Technical logs without personal data are processed for security and troubleshooting (art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest).

4. How long we keep the data

  • Account data: for as long as the account is active. When you delete the account, the account and its links are removed from the database.
  • User-generated data: tied to the account and deleted with it.
  • Anonymous statistics: 30 days, then automatically cleared.
  • Push tokens: until you disable push for a venue or delete the account.

5. Who we share data with

We sell nothing and we do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing. We use the following processors:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage. Server region eu-north-1 (Stockholm), within the EU/EEA.
  • Fly.io — hosting for the app's API.
  • Expo and Apple Push Notification Service — push delivery.
  • MiniMax (Nanonoble Pte. Ltd., Singapore) — the provider of the language model behind the agent chat.

Only the text you type, the interpreted request (date, budget, company, category) and a short list of event titles, times, categories and prices are sent to MiniMax. We do not send your user id, your e-mail address, your device id or your saved taste profile.

6. Transfers to third countries

MiniMax stores personal data, including submitted text, in a data centre in the United States. MiniMax states that transfers from the EU/EEA rely on EU–US Data Privacy Framework certification and, where applicable, the EU Commission standard contractual clauses (SCCs). Under MiniMax's API terms the provider may use submitted text to provide, maintain and improve the service, and their own policy states that personal data is retained for as long as needed for the stated purpose. The other processors (Supabase, Fly.io, Expo and APNs) process data within the EU/EEA.

7. Your rights

  • Access (art. 15) — write to hej@eventpulse.nu and we will send you a copy of your data.
  • Rectification (art. 16) — contact us if something is wrong.
  • Erasure (art. 17) — directly in the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • Restriction (art. 18) — turn off statistics and/or push under Profile → Privacy.
  • Portability (art. 20) — the same export by e-mail as for access.
  • Objection (art. 21) — contact us.

The most common rights can be exercised directly in the app under Profile. For anything else, write to hej@eventpulse.nu. We respond within 30 days as required by the GDPR.

8. Cookies and tracking

The website uses no cookies, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. The app uses no advertising networks and does not track you across other apps or websites.

9. Artificial intelligence

The app contains an AI agent that suggests events based on your question, and AI-generated images for event cards. The images are labelled “AI-generated”. The agent does not invent events: it picks among the events that actually exist in our database, with times, venues and ticket links from the organisers. Chats are sent to MiniMax as described in section 5.

10. Terms and age

You must be at least 18 years old to use EventPulse. Our terms of service apply in full.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes are announced in the app. The latest update is stated at the top.

12. Supervisory authority

If you believe we handle your data incorrectly you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) — imy.se.
