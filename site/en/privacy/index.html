Privacy

EventPulse is built on one principle: collect as little as possible, and sell nothing to anyone.

What we store

The public website (eventpulse.nu) sets no cookies and no sessions. It reads events directly from our database via public REST and renders them in your browser.

The app stores an anonymous user profile that can be upgraded to an account via Apple ID or magic link. No third-party tracking pixels.

Analytics

Statistics are anonymous and can be turned off at any time. We do not share data with advertisers and we sell nothing.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to request export or deletion of all data we hold about you. Email hej@eventpulse.nu.
